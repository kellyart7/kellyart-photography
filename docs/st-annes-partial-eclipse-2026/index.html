St Annes Partial Eclipse 2026

Lytham St Annes – Partial Solar Eclipse, 2026 On a clear August evening, the skies above beach became the stage for one of nature’s most extraordinary spectacles: the partial solar eclipse of 2026. As the Moon slowly moved across the face of the Sun, the familiar coastal light began to change. For a brief period, the landscape took on an unusual atmosphere — a subtle reminder of the immense scale and movement of our solar system. Photographing the eclipse from the Lancashire coast was a challenge in timing, preparation and patience. Thses images captures a fleeting alignment between the Earth, Moon and Sun — a moment that will not be repeated in quite the same way. For me, it was more than simply photographing an astronomical event. It was an opportunity to combine two lifelong interests: photography and the wonder of the universe.
