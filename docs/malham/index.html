Malham

Malham, Yorkshire Malham is a landscape that rewards patience. From the dramatic limestone of Malham Cove to the quiet waters and open spaces of the surrounding Dales, there is always something different to discover. These images explore Malham through light, atmosphere and mood — moments when the familiar landscape takes on a different character. From deep shadows and muted tones to the subtle blues of early morning and evening, the aim is not simply to record the landscape, but to capture how it feels.
