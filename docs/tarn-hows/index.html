Tarn Hows

Tarn Hows is one of the Lake District’s most celebrated and photogenic landscapes, with its tranquil waters surrounded by woodland and magnificent mountain scenery. The reflections across the tarn, particularly in the early morning or evening light, create wonderful opportunities for atmospheric landscape photography. Whether captured beneath dramatic skies, in autumn colour or shrouded in mist, Tarn Hows offers a timeless beauty that makes it a favourite location for photographers. A National Trust beauty spot of pine, water and mist.
