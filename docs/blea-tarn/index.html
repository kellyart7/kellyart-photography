Blea Tarn

A small, still tarn cradled beneath the Langdale Pikes. Everyone can get out into the fells safely on this gentle out-and-back trail. Enjoy brilliant mountain views of the Langdale Pikes and other surrounding peaks, framed by the peaceful tarn – and all without the big climbs and rocky scrambles.
