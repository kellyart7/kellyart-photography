Haweswater

A remote reservoir valley, wide skies and rock shorelines. Haweswater Reservoir lies in one of the Lake District’s most remote and atmospheric valleys, surrounded by rugged fells and dramatic open landscapes. Created in the 1930s to provide water for Manchester, the reservoir now forms a striking part of the landscape, with its winding shoreline and towering mountains offering endless opportunities for photography. Changing weather, mist, reflections and seasonal light can transform Haweswater from a tranquil scene into a dramatic and evocative landscape.
