Whalley Village

Whalley is a historic Lancashire village rich in character, with the River Calder, traditional buildings and the magnificent ruins of Whalley Abbey providing a wonderful sense of place. The abbey’s ancient stonework, peaceful grounds and surrounding village offer a wealth of photographic opportunities, capturing both the beauty of the landscape and the history that has shaped this special corner of the Ribble Valley.
