Elterwater & Langdale

Reed-fringed water beneath the Langdale Pikes.Nestled in the heart of the Langdale Valley, Elterwater is a beautiful and peaceful location surrounded by some of the Lake District’s most dramatic scenery. The tranquil river, ancient woodland, stone cottages and towering fells provide a wealth of photographic opportunities, while the constantly changing light and weather can transform the landscape from soft and atmospheric to bold and dramatic. It is a place that rewards patience, exploration and a keen eye for the beauty in every season.
