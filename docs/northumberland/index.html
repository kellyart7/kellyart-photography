Northumberland

The Northumberland coast is a dream location for photography, offering dramatic castles, sweeping beaches, rugged dunes and vast open skies. From the iconic silhouette of Bamburgh Castle to the atmospheric ruins of Dunstanburgh and the wildlife-rich Farne Islands, every stretch of coastline offers something different. The ever-changing light, tides and weather create endless opportunities for capturing powerful landscapes and memorable images throughout the year.
