Thirlmere

A dramatic reservoir valley with waterfalls and autumn colour. Thirlmere is one of the Lake District’s most dramatic and atmospheric lakes, stretching beneath the towering slopes of Helvellyn and the surrounding fells. Its long, tranquil waters, wooded shores and rugged mountain backdrop provide endless opportunities for landscape photography, particularly when the weather brings mist, reflections or dramatic skies. With its ever-changing mood and spectacular scenery, Thirlmere is a truly inspiring location for any photographer exploring the Lake District.
