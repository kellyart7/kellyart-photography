St Michael’s Mount

St Michael’s Mount is one of Cornwall’s most iconic and dramatic photographic locations, rising from the sea just off the coast at Marazion. The changing tides, which reveal and cover the historic causeway, create endless opportunities for composition, while the castle and its reflections provide a striking focal point. From dramatic sunsets to moody coastal weather, St Michael’s Mount is a location that never fails to inspire.
