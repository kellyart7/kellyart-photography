The Lone Tree

The lone tree is one of the most powerful subjects in landscape photography, offering simplicity, isolation and a strong sense of character. Set against an expansive landscape or dramatic sky, a single tree can become a natural focal point, drawing the viewer into the image while suggesting stories of resilience, solitude and the passage of time.
