Loughrigg Fell

Loughrigg Fell is a photographer’s paradise, offering some of the most spectacular panoramic views in the Lake District from its summit. With Windermere, Rydal Water, Grasmere and the surrounding fells stretching out below, the ever-changing light and weather create endless opportunities for dramatic landscape photography. From misty mornings to golden evening light, Loughrigg rewards every visit with a new perspective and an unforgettable view.
