Ullswater

Haweswater is one of the Lake District’s most dramatic and remote landscapes, where the vast reservoir winds through a rugged valley surrounded by imposing fells. The changing light, shifting weather and atmospheric mist create an ever-changing scene, making it a particularly rewarding location for landscape photography. From quiet reflections on the water to dramatic views across the surrounding mountains, Haweswater offers a sense of wilderness and solitude that is difficult to find elsewhere in the Lake District.
