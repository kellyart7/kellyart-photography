Black and White

Black and white photography strips an image back to its essential elements, allowing light, shadow, texture, shape and composition to take centre stage. Free from the distraction of colour, it can reveal mood, emotion and atmosphere in a uniquely powerful way, transforming both dramatic landscapes and everyday moments into timeless and expressive images.
