Buttermere

Lakeshore paths, mist and mirror-still water in the western fells. Buttermere is one of the Lake District’s most beautiful and photogenic landscapes, surrounded by dramatic fells, peaceful woodland and the still waters of the lake. Its ever-changing weather and light create wonderful opportunities for photography, from misty mornings and atmospheric reflections to dramatic skies and rich autumn colours. Whatever the season, Buttermere offers a landscape of natural beauty and endless photographic inspiration.
