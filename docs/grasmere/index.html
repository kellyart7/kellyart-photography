Grasmere

Grasmere is one of the Lake District’s most enchanting locations, where a peaceful lake sits beneath a dramatic amphitheatre of surrounding fells. Its calm waters, winding shoreline and ever-changing light provide wonderful opportunities for landscape photography throughout the year. The village itself has a rich history, with roots stretching back to the early medieval period, and is famously associated with the poet William Wordsworth, who made his home at nearby Dove Cottage in 1799 and drew much of his inspiration from the beauty of the surrounding landscape.
