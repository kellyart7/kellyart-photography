Panorama

Landscapes Beyond the Ordinary Frame My panoramic series explores the unique potential of the wide-format image, capturing landscapes in a way that reflects the scale and grandeur of the scene as experienced in person. By bringing together a wider field of view, panoramics allow sweeping compositions, dramatic skies and subtle details to work together, creating images that draw the viewer into the landscape and offer a greater sense of place.
