Padstow

Padstow is a charming Cornish harbour town, full of character, colour and photographic interest. From the working harbour and traditional fishing boats to the narrow streets and dramatic coastline nearby, the area offers a wonderful variety of subjects. The changing tides and coastal light make every visit an opportunity to capture something new.
